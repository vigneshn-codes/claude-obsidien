# Alex Hormozi — Author Profile

## Quick Facts
- **Full Name:** Alexander Hormozi
- **Born:** 1989, Townson, Maryland, USA
- **Nationality:** Iranian-American (first generation)
- **Education:** B.A., Corporate Strategy — Vanderbilt University (Magna Cum Laude)
- **Spouse / Business Partner:** Leila Hormozi (Co-founder & CEO, Acquisition.com)
- **Current Role:** Co-founder, Acquisition.com
- **Estimated Net Worth:** ~$100M–$200M (combined with Leila, publicly stated portfolio ~$100M+)

## Career Arc
1. **Management Consulting (post-grad, ~2 years)** — Worked at a strategy firm before leaving to start his own business.
2. **Gym Ownership (2013–2016)** — Opened his first brick-and-mortar gym. Famously slept on the gym floor when he could not pay rent. Eventually scaled to 6 locations grossing ~$10K/month each.
3. **Gym Launch (2017–2021)** — Co-founded with Leila Hormozi to license his gym-acquisition system. Helped 4,000+ gyms; sold a 66% stake in 2021 at a $46.2M valuation.
4. **Prestige Labs (2018)** — Sports nutrition supplement company; hit $1.7M/month revenue within 6 months.
5. **ALAN (2020)** — Software for customer acquisition in brick-and-mortar businesses.
6. **Acquisition.com (2020–present)** — Holding company that invests in and scales portfolio businesses. Reported $200M+ annual revenue by 2023.

## Public Persona
- One of the most-followed business educators on YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, and Threads.
- Known for blunt, no-fluff content: long-form business videos, framework breakdowns, and short-form "punchline" clips.
- Stated mission: give away most of the value for free so that businesses learn whether to join his portfolio.

## Published Books (the "$100M" Series)
1. **$100M Offers: How To Make Offers So Good People Feel Stupid Saying No** (2021)
2. **$100M Leads: How to Get Strangers To Want To Buy Your Stuff** (2023)
3. **$100M Money Models: How To Get Customers To Pay You More Than It Costs To Acquire Them** (2025)
4. **Gym Launch Secrets: The Step-By-Step Guide To Building A Massively Profitable Gym** (2019) — predecessor to the $100M series, niche-specific.

## Brand & Tone
- **Voice:** Direct, math-driven, contrarian.
- **Themes:** Volume, leverage, offer design, lifetime value, work ethic, long-term thinking.
- **Format:** Each book is structured as a self-contained "playbook" with frameworks, worked examples, and explicit step-by-step instructions.
