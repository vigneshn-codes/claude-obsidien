# $100M Leads: How to Get Strangers To Want To Buy Your Stuff

## Bibliographic Info
- **Author:** Alex Hormozi
- **Published:** August 2023
- **Series:** Acquisition.com $100M Series — Book 2
- **Subtitle:** *How to Get Strangers To Want To Buy Your Stuff*
- **Length:** ~280 pages

## Central Thesis
> "Leads are the lifeblood of your business. Without them, you'll never make a sale."

This book is a tactical playbook for generating "engaged" leads at scale. Hormozi defines a **lead** as someone who has demonstrated they *might* buy, and an **engaged lead** as a lead who is actively interacting with your business. Engaged leads are the only metric that matters.

## Book Structure

### Part I — Start Here
- **What Is a Lead?** Reframes the definition: not just contact info, but *engagement*. Counting raw lists is a vanity metric.
- **Lead Magnets — Get Leads To Come To You.** The single highest-leverage activity if you're stuck.

### Part II — Get Understood (The Lead Magnet System)
- **A lead magnet is a complete solution to a narrow problem** — it must be:
  - Solving a problem your customer has
  - Easy to consume
  - Valuable enough that someone *would pay for it*
- **Six types of lead magnets:** software, information, services, a sample of your product, a checklist/template, a community/event.
- **The Five Steps to Create a Lead Magnet:**
  1. Identify the ideal prospect
  2. Identify a narrow problem
  3. Pick the type
  4. Make it valuable
  5. Make it easy to consume
- **How to advertise it:** every piece of content, ad, email, and bio should funnel to the lead magnet.

### Part III — Get & Keep Attention (The Core Four)
The four — and only four — ways to get more leads:

| # | Channel | Audience | Mode |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Warm Outreach** | People who know you | 1-to-1 |
| 2 | **Post Free Content** | People who know you | 1-to-many |
| 3 | **Cold Outreach** | People who don't know you | 1-to-1 |
| 4 | **Paid Ads** | People who don't know you | 1-to-many |

#### 1. Warm Outreach (Chapter 6)
- Start with everyone you know. Don't pitch — **ask who they know** who needs the result.
- "Reactivate, reach out, ask for referrals" is the order of operations.
- Cheapest, fastest leads in the early stage of any business.

#### 2. Posting Free Content (Chapter 7)
- Document, don't create. Share what's worked for *you*.
- **Value per second** is the rule — pack value, cut filler.
- The point isn't to go viral; it's to be discoverable by the right people.

#### 3. Cold Outreach (Chapter 8)
- Build a list (scrape, buy, enrich), personalize the opener, and offer something valuable.
- Cold email, cold DM, cold call — sequencing and volume win.
- Aim for compliance and quality, but **volume beats cleverness** at the beginning.

#### 4. Paid Ads (Chapter 9)
- Two purposes: get leads to call (high-intent) and get leads on a list (low-intent, nurtured).
- Budget logic: spend up to the LTV-to-CAC ceiling. If unit economics work, more ads = more money.
- Hormozi's rule: when in doubt, increase ad spend before increasing creative complexity.

### Part IV — Get Lead Getters (Scaling the Core Four)
You can do the Core Four yourself — or you can empower **other people** to do them for you. The four types of lead getters:
1. **Customers** — referrals, reviews, word of mouth.
2. **Employees** — internal sales, SDRs, content team, paid media team.
3. **Agencies** — outsourced production for any of the Core Four.
4. **Affiliates** — commission-based partners with their own audience.

This is how a business goes from "founder hustles for leads" to "lead machine."

### Part V — Get It All Working (Systems)
- Daily plans, weekly metrics, monthly reviews.
- Sequencing: which channel to add next, and when.
- How to know when to hire, when to outsource, when to ad-spend.

## Key Frameworks & Rules

### The Rule of 100
Pick one of the following and do it every day for 100 days:
- Reach out to **100 people**
- Spend **100 minutes** creating content
- Spend **$100** on ads

> "If you reach out to 100 people total instead of 100 per day, you're doing 1/42 of the required effort."

### More, Better, New
If you want more leads, you have three options:
1. **More** of what you're already doing
2. **Better** at what you're already doing
3. **New** — add another of the Core Four

### Lead Magnet Hierarchy
The best lead magnet is one you'd actually charge for. "If you're struggling to get leads, make an amazing lead magnet."

### Engaged Leads Equation
`Engaged Leads = Reach × Lead-Magnet Conversion Rate × Show-Up Rate`

Each variable is independently optimizable.

This book is the second leg of the trilogy. Once you have a great **offer** (Book 1), you need **leads** to see it (Book 2). It then sets up **money models** (Book 3) — the unit economics that let you outspend competitors on lead acquisition.
