# $100M Money Models: How To Get Customers To Pay You More Than It Costs To Acquire Them

## Bibliographic Info
- **Author:** Alex Hormozi
- **Published:** August 16, 2025
- **Series:** Acquisition.com $100M Series — Book 3 (the trilogy's capstone)
- **Format:** Hardcover + companion 12-volume "playbook" binder for premium tiers
- **Time in Development:** ~4 years
- **Launch Event:** 9.5-hour livestream, 1M+ registrants, claimed $81M in 24-hour sales — reported as the largest 24-hour non-fiction book sale ever.

## Central Thesis
> "If you make more money from a customer in the first 30 days than it cost to acquire them, you can outspend every competitor — forever."

A *money model* is the upfront-cash architecture of your business: how you price, sequence, and stack offers so that **CAC is paid back fast** and you can reinvest aggressively into more lead generation. Where $100M Offers built the offer and $100M Leads filled the pipeline, $100M Money Models tunes the **economics**.

## Core Idea — The Four Constraints
Developed after working in-person with 1,026+ portfolio businesses, Hormozi identifies the four primary constraints that hold companies back. The book's structure maps systems to each:

1. **Leads constraint** — not enough qualified prospects.
2. **Sales constraint** — not enough closes.
3. **Delivery constraint** — customers don't stay long enough or pay enough (LTV / retention).
4. **Profit constraint** — the unit economics don't compound.

## Reported Chapter Highlights
The book is built around interlocking *systems*. Publicly previewed chapters include:
- **Chapter 8 — The Leads System:** unlocking unlimited prospects. For businesses that can't get enough qualified leads.
- **Chapter 10 — The Delivery System:** maximizing LTV and retention. For businesses where customers don't stay long enough or pay enough.
- Additional chapters covering pricing ladders, upsell/downsell sequencing, payment terms, and reinvestment loops.

## Key Concepts (Public Previews)
- **CAC Payback < 30 Days** — the north-star metric of a healthy money model.
- **Front-End / Back-End Stacking** — design a cheap or free front-end to maximize lead flow, monetize aggressively on the back-end.
- **Upfront-Cash Maximization** — pay-in-full bonuses, annual plans, deposits, advance payments.
- **LTV Lever Mechanics** — pricing increases, retention design, expansion offers, reactivation campaigns.
- **Compounding Reinvestment** — every dollar of profit goes back into the highest-ROI Core Four channel.
- **The ACQ AI Companion** — a chatbot trained on Hormozi's consulting playbook, bundled with the premium tiers.

## The Launch as Case Study
The launch is itself a $100M Money Models example in action:
- **The Offer:** "Donate 200 books" — buyers paid $5,998 to keep one copy and distribute 199. The CTA reframed buying as donating.
- **The Stack:** twelve physical playbooks in a premium binder + lifetime access to ACQ AI.
- **Upsell:** ACQ Scale Advisory at $3,000/month × 6 months = $18,000, including a private Skool community, six live virtual intensives (Marketing, Sales, LTV/Delivery, Profit, People/AI, Wealth), and direct access to Alex's advisory team.
- **Result (claimed):** $81M in 24 hours, biggest 24-hour non-fiction sale to date.

The launch demonstrates the book's own theses: high upfront cash, stacked perceived value, urgency/scarcity, premium pricing, and a back-end with stronger margins than the front-end.

## Why It Matters
This is the trilogy's "growth engine" book. Offers and leads are inputs; money models are the **multiplier**. Hormozi positions money model design as the difference between a business that grows linearly and one that compounds — and frames it as the single biggest reason most small businesses cannot afford to acquire customers profitably.
