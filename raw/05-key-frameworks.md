# Alex Hormozi — Key Frameworks (Cross-Book Reference)

A consolidated reference of the recurring mental models that appear across $100M Offers, $100M Leads, $100M Money Models, and Gym Launch Secrets.

---

## 1. The Value Equation
**Source:** $100M Offers, Chapter 6.

```
            Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement
Value  =  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                 Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice
```

| Lever | Direction | Practical Tactics |
|---|---|---|
| Dream Outcome | ↑ | Sell the transformation, not the deliverable. Articulate the *status* the customer will hold. |
| Perceived Likelihood | ↑ | Case studies, social proof, guarantees, credentials, *named mechanism*. |
| Time Delay | ↓ | Quick wins, fast onboarding, instant access, done-for-you components. |
| Effort & Sacrifice | ↓ | Templates, automations, removed prerequisites, simplified UX. |

**Insight:** because the equation divides, as the denominator approaches zero, perceived value approaches infinity. Perception > reality.

---

## 2. The Grand Slam Offer (5 Parts)
1. Dream Outcome.
2. Increased Likelihood of Achievement.
3. Decreased Time Delay.
4. Decreased Effort & Sacrifice.
5. Enhancers: Bonuses + Guarantees + Scarcity + Urgency + Naming.

**Construction Steps (Trim & Stack):**
1. Identify Dream Outcome.
2. List every Problem on the path.
3. Transform Problems into Solutions.
4. List Solution Delivery Vehicles.
5. **Trim** low-value/high-cost vehicles. **Stack** high-value/low-cost vehicles.

---

## 3. The Four Indicators of a Great Market
A pre-offer filter. The market must be:
1. **Massive Pain** — desperate need, not just a want.
2. **Purchasing Power** — money or access to money.
3. **Easy to Target** — reachable through identifiable channels.
4. **Growing** — tailwinds beat headwinds.

---

## 4. The Core Four (Lead Generation)
**Source:** $100M Leads.

|  | **1-to-1** | **1-to-many** |
|---|---|---|
| **Warm (know you)** | Warm Outreach | Post Free Content |
| **Cold (don't know you)** | Cold Outreach | Paid Ads |

These are the *only four* ways to get more attention. Every lead-gen activity collapses to one of these.

---

## 5. The Rule of 100
Pick one and do it daily for 100 days:
- Reach out to **100 people**, or
- Spend **100 minutes** creating content, or
- Spend **$100** on ads.

**The point:** most "doesn't work" claims come from doing 1/30th to 1/100th of the required volume.

---

## 6. More, Better, New
When stuck on leads or revenue, three knobs:
1. **More** of what you already do.
2. **Better** at what you already do.
3. **New** — add another Core Four channel.

Always exhaust *More* and *Better* before adding *New*.

---

## 7. The Four Lead Getters
Scale beyond your own time by recruiting others to run the Core Four:
1. **Customers** — referrals, reviews, word of mouth.
2. **Employees** — internal SDRs, content creators, paid media buyers.
3. **Agencies** — outsourced execution.
4. **Affiliates** — commission-based partners with their own audiences.

---

## 8. Lead Magnet Design (5 Steps)
1. Identify ideal prospect.
2. Identify a narrow problem.
3. Pick the type (software, info, service, sample, checklist/template, community/event).
4. Make it valuable enough that someone would *pay* for it.
5. Make it easy to consume.

---

## 9. Guarantee Taxonomy
- **Unconditional:** "Money back, no questions."
- **Conditional:** "Money back if you do X and don't get Y."
- **Anti-Guarantee:** "No refunds — here's why."
- **Implied / Performance:** revenue share, equity, performance-based pricing.

Stack multiple guarantee types; conditional + anti = strong filtering + risk reversal.

---

## 10. The Four Constraints (Money Models)
**Source:** $100M Money Models.

Every business is bottlenecked by one of:
1. **Leads** — not enough qualified prospects.
2. **Sales** — not enough closes.
3. **Delivery** — LTV/retention too low.
4. **Profit** — unit economics don't compound.

Diagnose the binding constraint first; do not optimize the others until it's relieved.

---

## 11. CAC-Payback Heuristic
> "If you make more in 30 days than CAC, you can outspend every competitor."

The defining number of a healthy money model. Front-end offers exist to drive this number down; back-end offers exist to multiply LTV.

---

## 12. Divergent vs Convergent Thinking
- **Convergent:** one right answer (math, code). Used in *execution*.
- **Divergent:** many right answers (design, marketing). Used in *offer creation*. Generate quantity → trim to quality.

---

## 13. The Naming MAGIC Formula
For naming offers/programs:
- **M**ake a magnetic reason
- **A**nnounce the avatar
- **G**ive them a goal
- **I**ndicate a timeframe
- **C**omplete with a container word (Challenge, Bootcamp, Intensive, Accelerator, Blueprint)

Example: *"The 6-Week Stubborn Belly Fat Challenge for Busy Moms."*

---

## 14. Standards & Identity Heuristic
> "We don't rise to the standards we have when others are watching; we fall to the standards we have when no one is."

Use private standards as the leading indicator of long-term outcomes. Audit the work nobody sees.

---

## 15. Skill Stacking Mental Model
> "Entrepreneurship is about acquiring skills, beliefs, and character traits."

Map the skills/beliefs/traits the next-level version of the business requires. Close those gaps first; outputs follow.
