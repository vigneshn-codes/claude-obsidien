# Concept — The Value Equation

**Source book:** $100M Offers, Chapter 6
**Type:** Core framework
**Related concepts:** Grand Slam Offer, Trim & Stack, Perceived Value

## Definition
The Value Equation is Hormozi's mathematical model for perceived value. It identifies the four variables that, when manipulated, control how valuable an offer feels to a prospect.

## The Formula
```
            Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement
Value  =  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                 Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice
```

## The Four Variables

### Numerator (increase)
- **Dream Outcome** — the felt experience the prospect imagines after success. The transformation, the status, the relief from pain.
- **Perceived Likelihood of Achievement** — how confident the prospect is that *this* offer will deliver. Driven by proof: case studies, guarantees, credentials, named mechanisms.

### Denominator (decrease)
- **Time Delay** — gap between purchase and benefit. Shorter = more valuable.
- **Effort & Sacrifice** — ancillary work, learning curve, lifestyle changes, decisions required.

## Why It Matters
Because the equation divides, value approaches infinity as the denominator approaches zero. This means **reducing friction is at least as powerful as amplifying outcome**.

## Worked Example (Weight Loss Coach)
- Bad offer: "12-week program, $200, you'll be healthier."
  - Dream Outcome: vague. Likelihood: low. Time: 12 weeks. Effort: high.
- Grand Slam version: "Lose 20 lbs in 6 weeks or your money back, fully done-for-you meal prep delivered, 1-on-1 coaching, takes 30 min/day."
  - Dream Outcome: specific. Likelihood: guaranteed. Time: 6 weeks. Effort: minimized.

## Hormozi's Famous Illustration
The London Underground installed arrival-time displays. Rider satisfaction increased more from *knowing the wait* than from actually shortening it. **Perception > reality.**

## How to Apply
1. Score your current offer 1–10 on each variable.
2. Identify the weakest variable.
3. Brainstorm 10 ways to improve just that one.
4. Re-score and ship.
