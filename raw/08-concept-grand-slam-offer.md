# Concept — Grand Slam Offer

**Source book:** $100M Offers
**Type:** Core framework
**Related concepts:** Value Equation, Trim & Stack, Bonuses, Guarantees

## Definition
A **Grand Slam Offer** is a unique, premium-priced, risk-reversed bundle of value so compelling that price comparison becomes impossible. The offer exists in its own category.

> "Make people an offer so good they would feel stupid saying no."

## The Five Parts
1. **Dream Outcome** — the result the customer truly wants (sold as transformation, not deliverable).
2. **Increased Likelihood of Achievement** — proof, guarantees, mechanism.
3. **Decreased Time Delay** — speed to first value.
4. **Decreased Effort & Sacrifice** — convenience, done-for-you.
5. **Enhancers** — bonuses, guarantees, scarcity, urgency, naming.

## Construction Process
1. Pick one niche.
2. Identify the Dream Outcome.
3. List **every problem** the prospect faces on the path.
4. Convert each problem into a Solution.
5. List Delivery Vehicles for each Solution.
6. **Trim** low-value/high-cost vehicles.
7. **Stack** high-perceived-value/low-cost vehicles.
8. Wrap in bonuses, guarantees, scarcity, urgency, and a magnetic name.

## Why It Works
- Removes price comparison — no like-for-like competitor.
- Inverts the buyer's risk — guarantees move risk onto the seller.
- Anchors high — premium price signals premium outcome.

## Worked Example Skeleton (B2B Coaching)
- **Dream Outcome:** add $50K MRR in 90 days
- **Likelihood:** 47 case studies, "double-your-money-back" guarantee
- **Time Delay:** week-one quick-win playbook
- **Effort/Sacrifice:** done-with-you SDR scripts + ad creative library
- **Enhancers:** limited to 10 seats/month (scarcity), $5K bonus expires Friday (urgency), name: *"The 90-Day Pipeline Accelerator"*

## Counter-Examples
A bad Grand Slam Offer is one where any individual piece could be bought elsewhere at the same price. If your offer survives a Google search, it isn't a Grand Slam Offer yet.
