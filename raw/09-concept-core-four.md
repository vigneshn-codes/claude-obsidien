# Concept — The Core Four (Lead Generation)

**Source book:** $100M Leads
**Type:** Core framework
**Related concepts:** Rule of 100, Lead Magnet, Lead Getters, More-Better-New

## Definition
The Core Four are the *only four* ways any business can get more leads. Every marketing tactic ever invented collapses into one of these.

|  | **1-to-1** | **1-to-many** |
|---|---|---|
| **Warm (audience knows you)** | Warm Outreach | Post Free Content |
| **Cold (audience doesn't know you)** | Cold Outreach | Paid Ads |

## The Four

### 1. Warm Outreach
Reach out to people who already know you: existing customers, past customers, your network. Don't pitch — **ask who they know** who needs the result.
- Order: reactivate → re-engage → request referrals.
- Cheapest lead source for any business under 24 months old.

### 2. Post Free Content
Share what's working — for yourself — with anyone watching.
- Document, don't create.
- Optimize **value per second**.
- Goal isn't virality — it's discoverability by the right people.

### 3. Cold Outreach
Reach out 1-to-1 to people who don't know you yet.
- Channels: email, DM, phone, LinkedIn.
- Levers: list quality, personalization, volume.
- **Volume beats cleverness** at the start.

### 4. Paid Ads
Pay platforms to put your offer in front of strangers.
- Two purposes: high-intent (call/buy now) or low-intent (capture + nurture).
- Spend up to the LTV-to-CAC ceiling.
- Hormozi's rule: when in doubt, *increase spend* before *increasing complexity*.

## Selection Logic
- **<$10K revenue/mo:** Warm Outreach + Free Content
- **$10K–$100K/mo:** Add Cold Outreach
- **$100K+/mo:** Add Paid Ads, then scale all four with Lead Getters

## Why the Quadrant Matters
Each cell has different unit economics, different time-to-first-lead, and different scaling ceilings. Most businesses overinvest in 1 cell and underinvest in the other 3. Mapping current activity to the quadrant exposes the gap instantly.
