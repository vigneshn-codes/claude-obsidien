# Concept — The Rule of 100

**Source book:** $100M Leads
**Type:** Behavioral rule / activity heuristic
**Related concepts:** Core Four, Volume Negates Luck

## Definition
Commit to **100 primary advertising actions every day for 100 days.** Pick one:
- Reach out to **100 people**
- Spend **100 minutes** creating content
- Spend **$100** on ads

## The Premise
> "If you reach out to 100 people total instead of 100 per day, you're doing 1/42 of the required effort."

Most "this doesn't work" claims come from dramatically under-dosing the input.

## Why 100?
- Round number → easy to remember and measure.
- High enough that randomness averages out (statistically significant sample).
- Low enough that one person can sustain it daily without staff.

## Why 100 Days?
- Long enough for cold pipelines to ripen.
- Long enough that creator content compounds.
- Long enough to disqualify the "I tried it once" cope.

## Application Patterns

| Stage | Recommended dose |
|---|---|
| Founder, no budget | 100 outreach messages/day |
| Founder + small audience | 100 minutes of content/day |
| Funded business | $100/day in ads (minimum) |
| Mature business | Run all three at higher volume via Lead Getters |

## Anti-Patterns Hormozi Warns Against
- Counting *attempts* not *contacts*.
- Mixing channels (50 emails + 50 DMs + 50 calls) when starting — pick one for clarity.
- Stopping at day 30 because "no one is biting." Most channels reward consistency over polish.

## The Companion Principle: Volume Negates Luck
Big enough sample size erases dependence on individual outcomes. A bad video doesn't matter when you post 90 more.
