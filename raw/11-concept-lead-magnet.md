# Concept — Lead Magnet

**Source book:** $100M Leads (Part II)
**Type:** Tactical asset
**Related concepts:** Core Four, Value Equation, Engaged Leads

## Definition
A **lead magnet** is a complete solution to a narrow problem, given away free in exchange for contact info and engagement.

## The Three Tests
A lead magnet works only if it:
1. Solves a **specific** problem the prospect actually has.
2. Is **immediately usable** (no setup gauntlet).
3. Is **valuable enough that someone would pay for it.**

If you wouldn't sell it, no one wants it for free.

## Six Types
1. **Software / tool** — calculator, template, mini-app.
2. **Information** — guide, ebook, video training, swipe file.
3. **Services** — free audit, free consultation, free done-for-you element.
4. **Sample of your product** — a "trial" piece of the core offer.
5. **Checklist / template** — operational shortcut.
6. **Community / event** — challenge, webinar, mini-cohort.

## The 5-Step Build
1. Identify the ideal prospect (specific avatar).
2. Identify a **narrow** problem (not "grow your business" — "write 5 cold emails that book meetings").
3. Pick the type (one of the six).
4. Make it valuable (would someone pay $50 for it? $500?).
5. Make it easy to consume (under 20 minutes to value).

## Conversion Sequence
```
Reach → Lead Magnet Hook → Opt-in → Delivery → Engagement → Offer
```
A lead is only "engaged" once they've **consumed** the lead magnet, not when they merely opted in.

## Common Failure Modes
- Too broad ("the ultimate guide to marketing").
- Too long (50-page PDF nobody reads).
- Solves a problem the prospect doesn't *yet* know they have.
- Solves a problem so completely that no upsell makes sense.

## Hormozi's Heuristic
> "If you're struggling to get leads, make an amazing lead magnet."

Most lead problems are downstream of a weak lead magnet, not a weak channel.
