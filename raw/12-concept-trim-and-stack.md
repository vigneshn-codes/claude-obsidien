# Concept — Trim & Stack

**Source book:** $100M Offers
**Type:** Offer-design technique
**Related concepts:** Grand Slam Offer, Divergent Thinking, Bonuses

## Definition
A two-step surgical method for designing the deliverable side of an offer:
- **Trim** the things that cost *you* a lot and deliver *little perceived value* to the customer.
- **Stack** the things that cost *you* little and deliver *high perceived value*.

## Why It Works
Most offers are accidental — they accumulate features by reflex. Trim & Stack imposes a 2×2 ROI lens:

|   | Low cost to you | High cost to you |
|---|---|---|
| **High value to customer** | ★ STACK | Keep selectively |
| **Low value to customer** | Optional filler | ✂︎ TRIM |

## Examples to Trim
- Endless 1-on-1 calls.
- Bespoke deliverables that don't scale.
- Long setup phases.
- Custom reports nobody reads.

## Examples to Stack
- Templates.
- Software / automations.
- Group coaching calls.
- Community access.
- Recordings of past programs.
- Pre-built playbooks.
- Limited-time bonus modules.

## The Mental Workflow
1. Brainstorm 20+ possible deliverables (divergent thinking).
2. Score each on the 2×2.
3. Trim the bottom-right.
4. Stack the top-left.
5. Re-bundle. Rename. Reprice.

## The Counter-Intuitive Insight
**Removing things often raises perceived value** when the removed things were friction (effort & sacrifice). A simpler offer with fewer high-value pieces beats a sprawling offer crammed with mediocre ones.
