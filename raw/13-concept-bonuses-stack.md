# Concept — Bonuses & The Value Stack

**Source book:** $100M Offers, Section IV
**Type:** Offer enhancer
**Related concepts:** Grand Slam Offer, Scarcity & Urgency, Guarantees

## Definition
Bonuses are additional solutions stacked on top of the core offer to multiply perceived value without proportionally raising delivery cost.

## Why Bonuses Beat Discounts
- A discount *reduces price*, anchoring you downward forever.
- A bonus *increases value*, keeping the price intact and the anchor high.
- Discounts attract price shoppers; bonuses attract value shoppers.

## How to Design a Great Bonus
Each bonus should:
1. Solve a **specific objection** ("but I won't have time" → bonus is a done-for-you template).
2. Have its own **distinct name**.
3. Have its own **ascribed dollar value**.
4. Ideally be a **tool, template, or shortcut** (low marginal cost, high perceived value).
5. Stack naturally with the core offer.

## The "Stack Slide" Format
A vertical list, each line:
- Bonus name
- One-sentence outcome
- Dollar value

End with:
- **Total Value: $X,XXX**
- **Your Price: $Y** (where Y is a small fraction of X)

## Bonus Source Library
- Cheat sheets / checklists
- Email / DM templates
- Pre-recorded mini-trainings
- 1:1 onboarding call
- Community access
- Recorded past cohorts
- Quarterly Q&A
- Refund-extension extras

## The Limit
Too many bonuses dilutes. Rule of thumb: enough bonuses that each major objection is neutralized, no more. If a bonus doesn't kill a specific objection, cut it.

## Hormozi's Heuristic
> "If the buyer can immediately answer 'what does this fix for me?' for every bonus on the slide, you've stacked correctly."
