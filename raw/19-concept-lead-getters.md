# Concept — The Four Lead Getters

**Source book:** $100M Leads, Part IV
**Type:** Scaling framework
**Related concepts:** Core Four, Rule of 100, More-Better-New

## Definition
**Lead getters** are other people who run the Core Four for you. You can do the Core Four yourself indefinitely — but the business is capped by your personal time. Lead getters break that cap.

## The Four Types

### 1. Customers
Existing customers generating new leads via:
- Referrals (one-to-one warm introductions)
- Reviews (one-to-many free content)
- Word of mouth (organic)
**Incentive levers:** referral rewards, contests, member-of-the-month, "bring a friend" bonuses.

### 2. Employees
Internal team members dedicated to one of the Core Four:
- SDRs / BDRs (cold outreach)
- Content creators (free content)
- Paid media buyers (ads)
- Account managers (warm reactivation)
**Incentive levers:** base + variable comp tied to lead volume and quality.

### 3. Agencies
Outsourced execution of any Core Four channel.
- Ad agencies, cold outreach agencies, content agencies, influencer agencies.
**Tradeoffs:** faster ramp, less control, higher per-unit cost, harder to internalize learning.

### 4. Affiliates
External partners with their own audiences who promote your offer for commission.
- Newsletter operators, podcasters, content creators, niche communities, complementary businesses.
**Incentive levers:** generous rev share, lifetime cookies, white-glove enablement.

## The Scaling Logic
| Stage | Best lead getter to add |
|---|---|
| Solopreneur | Customers (referrals) |
| First hires | Employees (1 channel owner) |
| $1M+ | Agencies (parallelize) |
| Strong brand | Affiliates (compound on brand) |

## Why All Four
Each lead getter has a different cost structure, scaling ceiling, and risk profile. Sophisticated businesses run all four simultaneously, with no single one accounting for >50% of leads — channel diversification = business durability.

## The Multiplier
With 4 lead-getter types × 4 Core Four channels = 16 distinct lead-acquisition surfaces. Most businesses run 1–2 of these 16 and wonder why growth is slow.
