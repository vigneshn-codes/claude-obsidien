# Concept — The MAGIC Naming Formula

**Source book:** $100M Offers, Section IV
**Type:** Copywriting / branding framework
**Related concepts:** Grand Slam Offer, Bonuses

## Definition
A 5-element formula for naming offers, programs, and lead magnets so they pre-sell themselves before any copy is read.

## The Acronym
- **M** — Make a magnetic reason
- **A** — Announce the avatar
- **G** — Give them a goal
- **I** — Indicate a timeframe
- **C** — Complete with a container word

## The Five Elements

### 1. Make a Magnetic Reason
Why now? Seasonal hooks, news events, milestones, frustrations. "Black Friday," "Q1 Kickoff," "Post-Pandemic Recovery."

### 2. Announce the Avatar
Name the audience explicitly. "For Busy Moms," "For First-Year Founders," "For Reps Hitting Quota Plateaus."

### 3. Give Them a Goal
The dream outcome, stated in concrete language. "Lose 20 lbs," "Add $50K MRR," "Book 10 demos a week."

### 4. Indicate a Timeframe
Make the result feel imminent. "In 6 Weeks," "By Year End," "Before Summer."

### 5. Complete with a Container Word
The package archetype. Different containers signal different intensities:
- **Challenge** — short, fun, transformational.
- **Bootcamp** — intense, immersive.
- **Intensive** — short, deep, high-touch.
- **Accelerator** — long, structured, outcomes-focused.
- **Blueprint** — DIY, framework-heavy.
- **Mastermind** — peer-driven, high-status.
- **Sprint** — focused, time-boxed.

## Worked Examples
- *"The 6-Week Stubborn Belly Fat Challenge for Busy Moms"*
  - M: stubborn (visceral) / G: lose belly fat / I: 6 weeks / A: busy moms / C: Challenge.
- *"The Q4 Pipeline Accelerator for Series-A SaaS Founders"*
- *"The 30-Day Cold Email Sprint for B2B Consultants"*

## Why It Works
A well-named offer answers four pre-objections in the headline alone:
1. Is this for me? (Avatar)
2. What will I get? (Goal)
3. How long? (Timeframe)
4. Is this real or a vague program? (Container)

That's most of the qualification work done before the prospect reads a single word of copy.

## The Container Mismatch Trap
Don't call something a "mastermind" if it's actually a course. Mismatch erodes trust faster than weak copy.
