# Company — Acquisition.com

**Founded:** 2020
**Founders:** Alex Hormozi (Co-founder), Leila Hormozi (Co-founder & CEO)
**Headquarters:** Las Vegas, Nevada (formerly Austin, TX)
**Type:** Private holding company / investment firm
**Reported Annual Revenue (2023):** $200M+

## What It Does
Acquisition.com invests in and scales privately-held businesses — typically $1M–$100M annual revenue companies with strong founders. Unlike a traditional PE firm, Acquisition.com:
- Takes minority or majority equity (not a fixed playbook).
- Provides hands-on operational support, not just capital.
- Specializes in customer acquisition, lead generation, and money model design — Hormozi's core IP.

## Portfolio Strategy
Hormozi has publicly stated the firm focuses on a small number of bets with deep involvement, rather than spray-and-pray. Stated portfolio criteria:
- Established business (post-product-market-fit).
- Recurring revenue or repeat customer model preferred.
- Founders willing to operate with discipline and accept advisory input.
- Industries where the Hormozi playbook (offers, leads, money models) is directly applicable: services, e-commerce, info products, education, fitness, B2B.

## Public Brand & Content Engine
Acquisition.com runs one of the most aggressive owned-media engines in B2B:
- **YouTube** — long-form business teardowns and frameworks.
- **The Game podcast** — short audio episodes (5–30 min).
- **Instagram / TikTok / Threads / X** — short-form clips repurposed from long-form.
- **Books** — the $100M trilogy as the brand flagship.

The stated philosophy: give away 99% of the value publicly. The book funnel and content brand attract inbound deal flow at a scale traditional dealmakers can't match.

## Relationship to the $100M Series
Each $100M book is structured around frameworks Acquisition.com uses internally with portfolio companies:
- $100M Offers → the offer-design system.
- $100M Leads → the lead-gen system.
- $100M Money Models → the money-model system.

Buying the books is the public on-ramp to Acquisition.com's funnel; advisory and equity engagement is the back-end.

## Notable Public Statements
- Stated goal: $1B portfolio EBITDA.
- Hormozi publicly takes no salary from Acquisition.com; income comes from equity.
- "Our edge isn't capital. Capital is commoditized. Our edge is operational playbooks."
