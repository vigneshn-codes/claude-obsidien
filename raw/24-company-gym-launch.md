# Company — Gym Launch

**Founded:** ~2017
**Founders:** Alex Hormozi, Leila Hormozi
**Industry:** Gym/fitness business licensing
**Exit:** 66% stake sold in 2021 at $46.2M valuation

## What It Did
Gym Launch licensed a complete client-acquisition system to gym owners. The package included:
- The **6-week challenge** front-end offer model.
- Done-for-you ad creative and funnel templates.
- Sales scripts and onboarding playbooks.
- Billing-cycle and pricing models designed to maximize upfront cash.
- Retention systems to fix the "hole in the bucket."

By the time of the exit, Gym Launch had worked with **4,000+ gyms** (some sources cite 5,000+), making it one of the largest licensing models in the boutique-fitness vertical.

## Origin Story
Hormozi opened his own gym ~2013, slept on the gym floor when rent ran out, and built a 6-location operation generating ~$10K/month per location. He converted his operational playbook into a portable system and began selling it to other gym owners via in-person workshops — the "Gym Launch tour."

## Why It Mattered for the Brand
Gym Launch is the field laboratory where every framework in the $100M series was originally pressure-tested:
- The **Grand Slam Offer** concept was first formalized via the 6-week challenge.
- The **Value Equation** was first articulated to explain why some challenge offers converted 10× others.
- **Money model** mechanics (paid-in-full bonuses, billing cycles, ascension paths) originated in gym operations.

The 4,000+ gyms became the data set that allowed Hormozi to generalize gym-specific tactics into the cross-industry $100M trilogy.

## Cultural Impact in Fitness
The Gym Launch model was widely copied and widely criticized in the boutique fitness world. Critics argued the 6-week challenge model:
- Pushed aggressive sales tactics on emotionally vulnerable buyers.
- Optimized for upfront cash at the expense of long-term member experience.

Supporters argued the model:
- Saved thousands of small gyms from bankruptcy.
- Brought professional-grade sales and ops to a notoriously under-managed industry.
- Created repeatable transformation outcomes for tens of thousands of end clients.

Hormozi addresses both critiques in *Gym Launch Secrets* and on podcast appearances.

## Sale & Aftermath
In 2021, a 66% stake in Gym Launch (and sister company Prestige Labs) was sold for $46.2M. The exit funded the founding of **Acquisition.com**, allowing Hormozi to generalize his work beyond the gym vertical.
