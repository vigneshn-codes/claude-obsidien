# Media — *The Game* Podcast

**Host:** Alex Hormozi
**Launched:** ~2020
**Format:** Solo monologues (most episodes), occasional interviews
**Typical Length:** 5–45 minutes
**Cadence:** Multi-episodes per week
**Distribution:** Apple Podcasts, Spotify, YouTube

## Concept
*The Game* is Hormozi's primary audio platform for delivering business frameworks in short, dense, monologue form. The stated promise: "How to make more money. How to keep more of it. How to enjoy it more."

Hormozi treats the podcast as a public lab notebook — many of the ideas later codified in the $100M trilogy debuted here in raw form before they were systematized into books.

## Episode Archetypes
1. **Framework explainer** — single concept (e.g., "The 3 Levers of Pricing").
2. **Field story** — a portfolio company case study with the lesson extracted.
3. **Mindset/identity** — reflections on discipline, focus, long-term thinking.
4. **Tactical teardown** — break down a specific funnel, offer, or campaign.
5. **Q&A clip** — repurposed from live events and YouTube.

## Why It Matters to the Books
The trilogy is essentially the *best of* the podcast, polished and structured:
- $100M Offers borrows heavily from earlier pricing/offer episodes.
- $100M Leads echoes years of lead-gen monologues.
- $100M Money Models pulls from unit-economics and CAC discussions developed publicly across hundreds of episodes.

For deep readers, the podcast back catalog functions as an extended commentary track on the books — same ideas, rougher edges, more examples.

## Production Style
- No fancy intros, sponsors, or fluff.
- Often recorded in a hotel room, gym, or office between meetings.
- Edited tight: dead air and tangents cut aggressively.
- Show notes minimal; the audio is the artifact.

## Strategic Role
The podcast is **top-of-funnel** for Acquisition.com:
- Builds trust over hundreds of free hours.
- Pre-qualifies sophisticated founders.
- Drives book sales and inbound advisory deal flow.
- Compounds over years — long-tail discovery on every episode.

## Notable Recurring Themes
- "Volume negates luck."
- "Skill acquisition is the highest-leverage activity."
- "Boredom is the price of mastery."
- "The best content is the one you actually published."
