# Timeline — Alex Hormozi's Career

A chronological view of the events, businesses, and books that produced the $100M series.

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| **1989** | Born in Townson, Maryland. |
| **~2007–2011** | Vanderbilt University. Graduates Magna Cum Laude, Corporate Strategy. |
| **~2011–2013** | Management consultant at a strategy firm (~2 years). |
| **~2013** | Quits to open his first gym. Sleeps on the gym floor when rent runs short. |
| **~2014–2016** | Scales to six gym locations, ~$10K/month each. |
| **~2016–2017** | Begins productizing his gym-acquisition playbook for other gym owners ("Gym Launch tour"). |
| **2017** | Co-founds **Gym Launch** with Leila Hormozi. Licenses the model nationally. |
| **2018** | Co-founds **Prestige Labs** (sports nutrition). Hits $1.7M/month within 6 months. |
| **2019** | Publishes **Gym Launch Secrets** — first book. |
| **2020** | Co-founds **ALAN** (software for brick-and-mortar customer acquisition). |
| **2020** | Founds **Acquisition.com** with Leila Hormozi as a holding company. |
| **2020** | Launches **The Game podcast**. |
| **2021** | Sells 66% of Gym Launch (and Prestige Labs) at a $46.2M valuation. |
| **2021** | Publishes **$100M Offers: How To Make Offers So Good People Feel Stupid Saying No** — first of the $100M trilogy. |
| **2022** | Scales personal brand aggressively across YouTube, Instagram, TikTok. |
| **2023** | Publishes **$100M Leads: How to Get Strangers To Want To Buy Your Stuff**. |
| **2023** | Acquisition.com reportedly crosses $200M annual revenue. |
| **2024** | Cumulative book sales reportedly exceed 1M copies. |
| **August 16, 2025** | Publishes **$100M Money Models**. 9.5-hour livestream launch event with 1M+ registrants. Claims $81M in 24-hour sales — reported as largest 24-hour non-fiction sale ever. |
| **Ongoing** | Acquisition.com continues to add portfolio companies; Hormozi continues weekly podcast and YouTube cadence. |

## Career Arc — In One Sentence
Consultant → gym owner → gym licensor → holding-company operator → public business educator → book-trilogy author → public-brand-as-distribution platform.
