# Glossary — Alex Hormozi Vocabulary

Quick-reference definitions for terms that recur across the $100M trilogy and Gym Launch Secrets. Listed alphabetically.

---

**Acquisition.com** — Hormozi's holding company (founded 2020). Invests in and operationally scales portfolio businesses. → `23-company-acquisition-com.md`

**Anti-Guarantee** — Explicitly stating "no refunds" with a transparent reason. Counterintuitively raises trust. → `15-concept-guarantee-taxonomy.md`

**Avatar** — The single, specific archetype of the ideal customer. Used in copy, naming, and offer design.

**Back-End** — Offers presented after the initial purchase: upsells, expansions, advisory, premium tiers. Source of LTV and profit.

**Bonus** — Additional solution stacked onto the core offer to multiply perceived value and neutralize specific objections. → `13-concept-bonuses-stack.md`

**CAC** — Customer Acquisition Cost. The total marketing + sales spend to acquire one customer.

**CAC Payback** — Time between spending CAC and earning it back in revenue/gross profit. Hormozi's target: <30 days. → `22-concept-cac-payback.md`

**Challenge** (offer container) — Time-bound transformation program. Hormozi's signature gym offer was the 6-week challenge.

**Conditional Guarantee** — Refund only if buyer fulfills defined effort and outcome conditions. Filters non-committed buyers. → `15-concept-guarantee-taxonomy.md`

**Container Word** — The package archetype in a program name (Challenge, Bootcamp, Intensive, Accelerator, Blueprint, Mastermind, Sprint). → `20-concept-magic-naming.md`

**Convergent Thinking** — One-right-answer cognition (math, code). Wrong mode for offer design. → `17-concept-divergent-thinking.md`

**Core Four** — The four — and only four — lead generation methods: Warm Outreach, Free Content, Cold Outreach, Paid Ads. → `09-concept-core-four.md`

**Divergent Thinking** — Many-right-answer cognition. Correct mode for offer design and naming. → `17-concept-divergent-thinking.md`

**Dream Outcome** — The customer's envisioned end state. The first variable in the Value Equation.

**Engaged Lead** — A lead who has *consumed* the lead magnet, not merely opted in. The only lead metric Hormozi treats as real.

**Four Constraints** — Leads, Sales, Delivery, Profit. Every business is bottlenecked by one. → `21-concept-four-constraints.md`

**Free Content** — One-to-many warm channel (one of the Core Four). Document what's worked for you.

**Front-End** — The entry-point offer optimized for low CAC and fast conversion. Designed to recover CAC before the back-end fires.

**Grand Slam Offer** — Unique, premium-priced, risk-reversed bundle so compelling price comparison is impossible. → `08-concept-grand-slam-offer.md`

**Gym Launch** — Hormozi's gym-licensing business (2017–2021). Sold for $46.2M valuation. → `24-company-gym-launch.md`

**Lead** — Anyone who has shown they *might* buy. A list of contact info is not yet leads — engagement is.

**Lead Getter** — Other people who run the Core Four for you: Customers, Employees, Agencies, Affiliates. → `19-concept-lead-getters.md`

**Lead Magnet** — A complete solution to a narrow problem, given away free. → `11-concept-lead-magnet.md`

**LTV** — Lifetime Value. Total gross profit a customer generates over their relationship with the business.

**MAGIC** — The 5-element naming formula: Magnetic reason / Avatar / Goal / Timeframe / Container. → `20-concept-magic-naming.md`

**Money Model** — The upfront-cash architecture of a business: how pricing, sequencing, and offers combine to recover CAC fast and fund growth. → `03-100m-money-models.md`

**More-Better-New** — The three knobs for fixing any growth problem, in order. → `18-concept-more-better-new.md`

**Niche** — A narrow, identifiable sub-market. Hormozi advocates "virtual monopoly" of a niche before expansion.

**Performance Guarantee** — Pay-on-results pricing (rev share, equity, performance fees). → `15-concept-guarantee-taxonomy.md`

**Rule of 100** — Commit to 100 outreach messages OR 100 minutes of content OR $100 in ads — every day, for 100 days. → `10-concept-rule-of-100.md`

**Scarcity** — Limit of *supply*. → `14-concept-scarcity-urgency.md`

**Starving Crowd** — A market with such demand that even mediocre offers succeed. Pick the crowd before the offer. → `16-concept-starving-crowd.md`

**Trim & Stack** — Two-step offer-design move: cut low-value/high-cost deliverables, add high-value/low-cost ones. → `12-concept-trim-and-stack.md`

**Unconditional Guarantee** — Full refund, no questions, any reason. Maximum risk reversal. → `15-concept-guarantee-taxonomy.md`

**Urgency** — Limit of *time*. → `14-concept-scarcity-urgency.md`

**Value Equation** — `Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)`. → `07-concept-value-equation.md`

**Volume Negates Luck** — Recurring Hormozi maxim. Large sample sizes erase dependence on any single outcome.

**Warm Outreach** — One-to-one warm channel (one of the Core Four). Start with everyone you know; ask who they know.
