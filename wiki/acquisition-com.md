## What It Is

A private holding company (founded 2020, Las Vegas) that invests in and scales privately-held businesses, typically at $1M–$100M annual revenue. Unlike traditional PE firms, Acquisition.com:

- Provides hands-on operational support alongside capital
- Specializes in customer acquisition, lead generation, and money model design (Hormozi's core IP)
- Takes minority or majority equity depending on the deal
- Reported $200M+ annual portfolio revenue by 2023

---

## How It's Different from Traditional PE

| Traditional PE | Acquisition.com |
|---|---|
| Financial engineering | Operational playbooks |
| Capital as edge | Offers + Leads + Money Models as edge |
| Passive board-level involvement | Hands-on, embedded operational support |
| Large portfolio, thin involvement | Small portfolio, deep involvement |

> "Our edge isn't capital. Capital is commoditized. Our edge is operational playbooks."

## The Content-as-Distribution Model

Acquisition.com runs one of the most aggressive owned-media engines in B2B:

- **YouTube** — long-form business teardowns and framework explainers
- **The Game podcast** — short dense audio episodes (see [[the-game-podcast]])
- **Instagram / TikTok / Threads / X** — short-form clips repurposed from long-form
- **$100M book trilogy** — the brand flagship and highest-leverage content asset

The model: give away the frameworks publicly → attract inbound deal flow from qualified founders → advisory and equity engagement is the back-end.

This is the [[money-model]] principle applied to a holding company: free content is the front-end, portfolio investment is the back-end.

## Portfolio Criteria (Stated)
- Established business (post product-market-fit)
- Recurring revenue or repeat customer model preferred
- Founder willing to accept advisory input and operate with discipline
- Industry where the Hormozi playbook is directly applicable
