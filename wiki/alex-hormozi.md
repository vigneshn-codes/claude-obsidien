**Summary:** Iranian-American entrepreneur, author of the $100M trilogy, co-founder of Acquisition.com — built his empire from sleeping on a gym floor to a reported $200M+ annual revenue holding company.

## Quick Facts

- **Full Name:** Alexander Hormozi
- **Born:** 1989, Townson, Maryland (Iranian-American, first generation)
- **Education:** B.A. Corporate Strategy, Vanderbilt University (Magna Cum Laude)
- **Spouse / Partner:** Leila Hormozi (Co-founder & CEO, Acquisition.com)
- **Current Role:** Co-founder, Acquisition.com
- **Estimated Net Worth:** ~$100M–$200M+ (combined with Leila)

## Brand Voice & Philosophy

- **Voice:** Direct, math-driven, contrarian, no-fluff
- **Core themes:** Volume, leverage, offer design, LTV, work ethic, long-term thinking
- **Format:** Each book is a self-contained "playbook" — frameworks, worked examples, step-by-step instructions
- **Content model:** Give away 99% of the value for free (YouTube, podcast, social); the book and advisory are the back-end
