# Core Four (Lead Generation)

**Summary:** The only four ways any business can get more leads — every marketing tactic ever invented collapses into one of these four channels.

**Category:** Lead Generation
**Sources:** `09-concept-core-four.md`, `02-100m-leads.md`, `05-key-frameworks.md`

## The Matrix

|  | **1-to-1** | **1-to-many** |
|---|---|---|
| **Warm (audience knows you)** | Warm Outreach | Post Free Content |
| **Cold (audience doesn't know you)** | Cold Outreach | Paid Ads |

These four cells are exhaustive. If someone claims to have a "new" marketing strategy, it lives in one of these four cells.

---

## The Four Channels in Detail

### 1. Warm Outreach (1-to-1, Warm)
Reach out to people who already know you: past customers, existing customers, your network.

- **Order of operations:** Reactivate → Re-engage → Request referrals
- Don't pitch — **ask who they know** who needs the result
- Cheapest, fastest leads for any business under 24 months old
- Example: "Hey [name], I'm helping [avatar] with [problem]. Do you know anyone who's been struggling with this?"

### 2. Post Free Content (1-to-many, Warm)
Share what's actually working — for yourself — with anyone watching.

- Document, don't create. Your own journey *is* the content.
- Optimize for **value per second** — cut filler ruthlessly
- Goal isn't virality — it's discoverability by the *right* people
- Channels: YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, newsletters, podcasts

### 3. Cold Outreach (1-to-1, Cold)
Reach out 1-to-1 to people who have never heard of you.

- Channels: email, DM, phone, LinkedIn
- Levers: list quality, personalization, volume
- **Volume beats cleverness** at the start — focus on quantity first, then optimize copy
- Personalize the opener, then offer something genuinely valuable (not "can I pick your brain")

### 4. Paid Ads (1-to-many, Cold)
Pay platforms to put your offer in front of targeted strangers.

- Two purposes: high-intent (buy/call now) or low-intent (capture + nurture)
- Spend up to the LTV-to-CAC ceiling (see [[cac-payback]])
- **When in doubt, increase spend before increasing creative complexity**
- Channels: Meta (FB/IG), Google, YouTube, TikTok, Pinterest, native, Reddit

## Selection Logic by Business Stage

| Revenue Stage | Primary Channel | Secondary Channel |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-revenue / $0–$10K/mo | Warm Outreach | Free Content |
| $10K–$100K/mo | + Cold Outreach | Refine above two |
| $100K+/mo | + Paid Ads | Scale all with [[lead-getters]] |

Do not add a new channel until the current one is maxed (see [[more-better-new]]).

---

## Why the Quadrant Matters

Each cell has:
- **Different unit economics** (warm outreach costs time; paid ads cost money)
- **Different time-to-first-lead** (warm = hours; content = weeks; cold = days; paid = hours-to-days)
- **Different scaling ceilings** (warm caps at your network; paid uncaps at your LTV)

Most businesses overinvest in 1 cell and underinvest in the other 3. Plotting current marketing activity on the 2×2 exposes the gap instantly.

## Application to Workout / Fitness Software

| Channel | Fitness/Software Specific Tactics |
|---|---|
| Warm Outreach | DM every personal trainer you know; ask if their clients need an app |
| Free Content | Post workout programming tips, PR breakdowns, form check teardowns |
| Cold Outreach | Email boutique gym owners about your software; DM fitness influencers with mid-size audiences |
| Paid Ads | Meta ads targeting gym-goers 25–40 with interest in specific lifts |
