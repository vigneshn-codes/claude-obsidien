# Engaged Leads

**Summary:** Hormozi's redefinition of what counts as a real lead — not a contact in a list, but someone who has actively consumed your lead magnet or interacted with your content.

**Category:** Lead Generation
**Sources:** `02-100m-leads.md`, `29-glossary.md`
**Related:** [[lead-magnet]], [[core-four]], [[rule-of-100]]

---

## The Redefinition

Most businesses count **raw contacts** as leads. Hormozi argues this is a vanity metric.

> "Engagement is the only lead metric that matters. The rest is vanity."

| What Most People Count | What Actually Matters |
|---|---|
| Email list size | People who opened AND clicked |
| Opt-ins | People who consumed the lead magnet |
| Followers | People who DM'd or replied |
| Impressions | People who took a defined action |
| "Traffic" | People who spent meaningful time |

---

## The Engaged Leads Formula

```
Engaged Leads = Reach × Lead-Magnet Conversion Rate × Consumption Rate
```

Each variable is independently optimizable:
- **Reach** → improve via [[core-four]] and [[lead-getters]]
- **Lead-Magnet Conversion Rate** → improve offer clarity, targeting, and hook
- **Consumption Rate** → improve delivery format, length, and time-to-value

---

## Why It Matters

Engaged leads convert at dramatically higher rates than cold contacts because:
1. They've already received value → reciprocity bias
2. They've already consumed time → sunk cost bias (in your favor)
3. They've self-selected as interested → warmer qualification
4. You have evidence of their problem → more specific follow-up

A list of 100 engaged leads often outperforms a list of 10,000 opt-ins.

---

## The Opt-In ≠ Lead Trap

The classic mistake: celebrate opt-in volume, then wonder why email sequences convert at 0.1%.

If someone downloads a PDF but doesn't open it, they are:
- A contact ✓
- An engaged lead ✗

Redesign the lead magnet delivery sequence to force consumption:
1. Deliver the magnet immediately (low time delay)
2. Give a specific instruction: "Start with Step 1 on page 3"
3. Follow up with a check-in: "Did you try the first workout? Here's what most people get wrong on day 2"
4. Only pitch after confirming consumption

## Application to Fitness / Workout Software

| Lead Magnet | Opt-in | Engaged (Consumed) |
|---|---|---|
| Free workout template PDF | Downloaded | Logged first workout using it |
| Free 7-day app trial | Signed up | Completed day-1 workout in app |
| Free 1RM calculator | Used calculator | Entered results + returned next week |
| Free webinar | Registered | Attended live OR watched replay >50% |
