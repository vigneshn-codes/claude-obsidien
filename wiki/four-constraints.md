# Four Constraints

**Summary:** Every business is bottlenecked by exactly one of four constraints at any given time — diagnosing the right one determines what to work on next.

**Category:** Money Models
**Sources:** `21-concept-four-constraints.md`, `03-100m-money-models.md`, `05-key-frameworks.md`
**Related:** [[cac-payback]], [[lead-getters]], [[more-better-new]], [[money-model]]

## The Four Constraints

| # | Constraint | Symptom |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Leads** | Not enough qualified prospects entering the system |
| 2 | **Sales** | Leads arrive but don't convert to customers |
| 3 | **Delivery** | Customers buy but don't stay or expand (low LTV, high churn) |
| 4 | **Profit** | Revenue flows but unit economics don't compound |

Working on a non-binding constraint produces **no growth**. A business with a delivery constraint cannot be fixed by more ads.

---

## The Diagnostic Sequence

Ask in order. The first "no" is your binding constraint.

```
1. Do we have enough leads coming in?
   → No: LEADS constraint. Fix the Core Four and Lead Getters.

2. Are those leads converting to customers?
   → No: SALES constraint. Fix the offer, copy, or sales process.

3. Are customers staying long enough and spending enough?
   → No: DELIVERY constraint. Fix onboarding, retention, LTV mechanics.

4. Is each revenue dollar producing compounding profit?
   → No: PROFIT constraint. Fix the money model, pricing, or CAC payback.
```

## Constraint Migration

Solving one constraint exposes the next. A business that fixes a leads constraint will quickly become sales-constrained. This is **healthy** — the business is growing into a new bottleneck. The task is to recognize the migration and re-run the diagnostic.

```
Leads → Sales → Delivery → Profit → Scale
(then back to Leads at higher volume)
```

---

## The Anti-Pattern

Building elaborate systems for the constraint you *enjoy* working on instead of the one that's actually binding.

- Founders with marketing backgrounds over-invest in leads when the real problem is churn
- Founders with ops backgrounds over-invest in delivery when the real problem is leads
- Technical founders over-invest in product features when the real problem is sales

Discipline means running the diagnostic and working on what it reveals, not what feels comfortable.

|---|---|---|
| App built, no users | Leads | [[rule-of-100]] — pick one Core Four channel, do it daily |
| Traffic to landing page, zero sign-ups | Sales | Rewrite offer with [[grand-slam-offer]] framework |
| Users sign up, cancel in month 2 | Delivery | Add Day-1 quick win; improve onboarding |
| Growing revenue, shrinking bank account | Profit | Audit CAC and LTV; run [[cac-payback]] math |
