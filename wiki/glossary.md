# Glossary

**Summary:** Quick-reference definitions for all Hormozi vocabulary that recurs across the $100M trilogy and Gym Launch Secrets.

**Category:** Vocabulary
**Sources:** `29-glossary.md`
**Related:** All concept pages

---

| Term | Definition | Wiki Page |
|---|---|---|
| **Acquisition.com** | Hormozi's holding company (2020–). Invests in and operationally scales portfolio businesses. | [[acquisition-com]] |
| **Anti-Guarantee** | Explicitly stating "no refunds" with a transparent reason. Counterintuitively raises trust when paired with strong proof. | [[guarantee-taxonomy]] |
| **Avatar** | The single, specific archetype of the ideal customer. Used in copy, naming, and offer design. | [[starving-crowd]] |
| **Back-End** | Offers presented after the initial purchase: upsells, expansions, advisory, premium tiers. Source of LTV and profit. | [[money-model]] |
| **Bonus** | Additional solution stacked on the core offer to multiply perceived value and kill specific objections. | [[bonuses-stack]] |
| **CAC** | Customer Acquisition Cost. Total marketing + sales spend to acquire one customer. | [[cac-payback]] |
| **CAC Payback** | Time between spending CAC and earning it back in gross profit. Hormozi's target: < 30 days. | [[cac-payback]] |
| **Challenge** | Time-bound transformation program. Hormozi's signature gym offer was the 6-week challenge. | [[six-week-challenge]] |
| **Conditional Guarantee** | Refund only if buyer fulfills defined effort and outcome conditions. Filters non-committed buyers. | [[guarantee-taxonomy]] |
| **Container Word** | Package archetype in a program name (Challenge, Bootcamp, Intensive, Accelerator, Blueprint, Mastermind, Sprint). | [[magic-naming]] |
| **Convergent Thinking** | One-right-answer cognition (math, code). Wrong mode for offer design. | [[divergent-thinking]] |
| **Core Four** | The four — and only four — lead generation methods: Warm Outreach, Free Content, Cold Outreach, Paid Ads. | [[core-four]] |
| **Divergent Thinking** | Many-right-answer cognition. Correct mode for offer design and naming. | [[divergent-thinking]] |
| **Dream Outcome** | The customer's envisioned end state. First variable in the Value Equation. | [[value-equation]] |
| **Engaged Lead** | A lead who has *consumed* the lead magnet, not merely opted in. The only lead metric that matters. | [[lead-magnet]] |
| **Four Constraints** | Leads, Sales, Delivery, Profit. Every business is bottlenecked by exactly one at any time. | [[four-constraints]] |
| **Front-End** | Entry-point offer optimized for low CAC and fast conversion. Designed to recover CAC before back-end fires. | [[money-model]] |
| **Grand Slam Offer** | Unique, premium-priced, risk-reversed bundle so compelling that price comparison becomes impossible. | [[grand-slam-offer]] |
| **Gym Launch** | Hormozi's gym-licensing business (2017–2021). Sold for $46.2M valuation. | [[gym-launch-company]] |
| **Lead** | Anyone who has shown they *might* buy. A list of contact info is not leads — engagement is. | [[lead-magnet]] |
| **Lead Getter** | Other people who run the Core Four for you: Customers, Employees, Agencies, Affiliates. | [[lead-getters]] |
| **Lead Magnet** | A complete solution to a narrow problem, given away free. | [[lead-magnet]] |
| **LTV** | Lifetime Value. Total gross profit a customer generates over the full relationship. | [[cac-payback]] |
| **MAGIC** | The 5-element naming formula: Magnetic reason / Avatar / Goal / Timeframe / Container. | [[magic-naming]] |
| **Money Model** | The upfront-cash architecture: how pricing, sequencing, and offers combine to recover CAC fast and fund growth. | [[money-model]] |
| **More-Better-New** | Three knobs for any growth problem, in order: do more, do it better, add something new. | [[more-better-new]] |
| **Niche** | A narrow, identifiable sub-market. Hormozi advocates "virtual monopoly" before expansion. | [[starving-crowd]] |
| **Performance Guarantee** | Pay-on-results pricing (rev share, equity, performance fees). | [[guarantee-taxonomy]] |
| **Rule of 100** | Commit to 100 outreach messages OR 100 min content OR $100 in ads — every day, for 100 days. | [[rule-of-100]] |
| **Scarcity** | Limit of *supply*. | [[scarcity-urgency]] |
| **Starving Crowd** | A market with such demand that even mediocre offers succeed. Pick the crowd before the offer. | [[starving-crowd]] |
| **Trim & Stack** | Cut low-value/high-cost deliverables; add high-value/low-cost ones. | [[trim-and-stack]] |
| **Unconditional Guarantee** | Full refund, no questions, any reason. Maximum risk reversal. | [[guarantee-taxonomy]] |
| **Urgency** | Limit of *time*. | [[scarcity-urgency]] |
| **Value Equation** | `Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)` | [[value-equation]] |
| **Volume Negates Luck** | Large sample sizes erase dependence on any single outcome. | [[rule-of-100]] |
| **Warm Outreach** | 1-to-1 warm channel. Start with everyone you know; ask who they know. | [[core-four]] |
