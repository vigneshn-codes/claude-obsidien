# Guarantee Taxonomy

**Summary:** A four-type framework for risk-reversal mechanisms that shift perceived risk from buyer to seller — the highest-ROI single enhancer in offer design.

**Category:** Offer Design
**Sources:** `15-concept-guarantee-taxonomy.md`, `01-100m-offers.md`, `05-key-frameworks.md`
**Related:** [[grand-slam-offer]], [[value-equation]], [[bonuses-stack]], [[scarcity-urgency]]

## Why Guarantees Matter

The buyer's core fear is: *"What if this doesn't work for me?"* A guarantee answers that fear before they have to ask. Hormozi argues guarantees are the highest-ROI single enhancer because:

- They increase conversion on the front end.
- They decrease buyer anxiety, which increases follow-through, which increases results, which decreases refund requests — a self-reinforcing loop.
- They signal that the seller believes in the product.

> "If you're not willing to guarantee an outcome, you don't believe in it. Fix the offer until you can."

---

## The Four Types

### 1. Unconditional Guarantee
**"Full money back, no questions, any reason."**

- Best for: entry-level products, low-to-mid price points, mass-market
- Highest trust signal
- Highest refund exposure — attracts tire-kickers at scale
- Example: "If you don't love it in 30 days, we'll refund you instantly — no forms, no call required."

### 2. Conditional Guarantee
**"Money back if you do X and don't get Y."**

- Best for: coaching programs, group cohorts, high-ticket services
- Filters non-committed buyers (only serious buyers fulfill the conditions)
- Conditions must be **clear**, **trackable**, and **achievable**
- Premium variants:
  - **Double-your-money-back** if conditions are met
  - **Refund + keep the bonuses** — removes all risk of "I'll lose what I paid for"
- Example: "Complete all 4 workouts/week and log every session. If you don't hit a new PR in 30 days, we'll refund every penny."

### 3. Anti-Guarantee
**"No refunds — here's why."**

- Best for: consumed information products, live events, custom builds
- Must be paired with a transparent, believable reason
- Counterintuitively *raises* trust when paired with strong proof and testimonials
- Example: "We don't offer refunds — once you've watched the training, the knowledge is yours. But that's why we've documented 600 success stories before asking you to invest."

### 4. Implied / Performance Guarantee
**"You pay only when you get results."**

- Best for: high-trust B2B, consulting, agency services, equity deals
- Revenue share, performance fees, equity stakes
- Eliminates upfront buyer risk entirely
- Example: "We take 20% of the new revenue we generate for your gym. You pay nothing until we deliver."

## Stacking Guarantees

Multiple types can layer on a single offer:

```
30-day unconditional refund
+ 90-day conditional double-your-money-back (if you complete all sessions)
+ Anti-guarantee on consumed bonus materials
```

Each layer covers a different buyer objection at a different stage of the relationship.

---

## The Effort Multiplier

Longer guarantee windows correlate with **higher conversion** and **lower refund rates** — because long windows attract committed buyers who actually use the product. A 365-day guarantee consistently outperforms a 30-day guarantee in refund rate per customer, despite more exposure time.

---

## Common Failure Modes

| Failure | Why It Breaks |
|---|---|
| Vague conditions ("if you really try") | Unenforceable; buyer and seller will disagree |
| Hidden refund process | Trust destroyer — feels like a trap |
| Guarantee stronger than operation can support | Cash flow crisis if conversion spikes + refunds spike |
| No guarantee at all | Leaves the buyer's core fear unanswered |
