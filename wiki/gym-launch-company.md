# Gym Launch (Company)

**Summary:** Hormozi's gym-licensing business (2017–2021) that worked with 4,000+ gyms and was sold at a $46.2M valuation — the field laboratory for every framework in the $100M series.

**Category:** Author & Companies
**Sources:** `24-company-gym-launch.md`, `00-author-alex-hormozi.md`
**Related:** [[alex-hormozi]], [[acquisition-com]], [[gym-launch-model]], [[six-week-challenge]]

---

## What It Did

Gym Launch licensed a complete client-acquisition system to gym owners. The package:
- The [[six-week-challenge]] front-end offer model
- Done-for-you Facebook/Instagram ad creative and funnel templates
- Sales scripts and objection-handling playbooks
- Billing-cycle and pricing models (upfront cash maximization)
- Retention systems to fix the attrition problem

**Scale:** 4,000–5,000 gyms by 2021 exit.

---

## Origin Story

Hormozi opened his first gym around 2013 after leaving management consulting. He famously slept on the gym floor when cash ran out. By 2016 he had 6 locations running at ~$10K/month each. He then systematized the acquisition model and began selling it to other gym owners via in-person "Gym Launch tours" starting around 2016–2017.

---

## Why It Matters for the $100M Framework

Gym Launch is where every $100M concept was first stress-tested:

| Framework | Origin in Gym Launch |
|---|---|
| Grand Slam Offer | 6-week challenge — studied why some converted 10× others |
| Value Equation | First articulated to explain those conversion differences |
| Money model / CAC payback | Challenge fee covers ad cost (front-end pays back CAC) |
| Conditional guarantee | "Money back if you complete every session and don't lose X lbs" |
| MAGIC naming | "The 6-Week Body Transformation Challenge for [City] Residents" |

The 4,000+ gyms became the data set allowing Hormozi to generalize niche tactics into the cross-industry $100M trilogy.

---

## The Exit

- 2021: Sold 66% of Gym Launch (and Prestige Labs) for a **$46.2M valuation**
- Exit funded the founding of Acquisition.com
- Allowed Hormozi to generalize his work beyond the gym vertical

## Cultural Impact in Fitness

**Critics argued the model:**
- Pushed aggressive sales tactics on emotionally vulnerable buyers
- Optimized for upfront cash at the expense of member experience

**Supporters argued:**
- Saved thousands of gyms from bankruptcy
- Brought professional-grade operations to an under-managed industry
- Created repeatable transformation outcomes for tens of thousands of end clients
