## The Three Problems It Solves

Every failing gym has at least one of these:

| Problem | Symptom | System |
|---|---|---|
| Broken client acquisition | Can't reliably bring in new members | Front-end offer (see [[six-week-challenge]]) |
| Broken revenue model | Pricing, billing, and packaging leave money on the table | Revenue model redesign |
| Hole in the bucket (attrition) | Clients churn faster than they're acquired | Retention & onboarding system |

Hormozi's rule: *fix acquisition first*. Without leads, nothing else matters.

---

## The Client Acquisition System

**The 6-Week Challenge as Front-End:**
- Specific transformation outcome → specific avatar → specific name
- Paid upfront (recovers CAC before back-end fires)
- Built-in conversion to membership at the end

**Lead Sources:**
1. Paid Facebook/Instagram ads (primary paid channel)
2. Referrals (customer lead getter)
3. Neighborhood outreach and local events (warm outreach)

**Sales Process:**
- "Preframing" tactic: ask qualifying questions that position you as busy and selective
- Consultative close: prospect says yes to *themselves* before price is mentioned
- Scripted objection handling: price, time, spouse approval, "let me think about it"

## The Revenue Model

**Billing Cycle Methodology:**
- Move away from month-to-month
- Offer annual plans with pay-in-full incentives (30–40% discount)
- Multi-month upfront commitments maximize cash on hand
- Reduces churn-driven revenue loss (harder to cancel when paid upfront)

**Pricing Architecture:**
- Charge premium over commercial gyms
- Justify with: specific results, accountability, community, named protocols
- Entry: 6-week challenge → Monthly membership → Coaching tier → Specialty programs

## The Retention System ("Stick")

The first 14 days predict long-term retention. Every retention system starts on Day 1.

**Onboarding Rituals:**
- Photo + baseline measurements on Day 1 (commitment device + future social proof)
- Personal introduction to community
- "First win" designed into the first session
- Welcome gift or swag

**Ongoing Retention:**
- Recurring 1:1 goal-setting reviews (re-upping commitment)
- Group identity mechanics (team names, friendly competitions)
- Social accountability (public progress, workout partners)
- Missed-session reactivation (call or text within 24–48 hours)

## Why It Mattered Beyond Gyms

Gym Launch is the prototype for the entire $100M series:
- The Grand Slam Offer concept came from studying why some 6-week challenges converted 10× others
- The Value Equation was first articulated to explain those differences
- CAC payback math (challenge fee covers ad cost) preceded the $100M Money Models framework
- 4,000+ gyms = the data set that validated generalizing gym-specific tactics across industries

## Operating in a Non-Gym Fitness Business

Any local or digital service with these properties can run a gym-launch-style model:
- Transformation outcome that's visible in 4–8 weeks
- Repeat purchase or subscription makes sense afterward
- Accountability increases results (and therefore reduces refund rates and churn)

Examples: martial arts, CrossFit, dance studios, yoga studios, personal training, running clubs, online fitness coaching.
