# Lead Getters

**Summary:** Other people who run the Core Four for you — the mechanism by which a business breaks the founder's personal time ceiling on lead generation.

**Category:** Lead Generation
**Sources:** `19-concept-lead-getters.md`, `02-100m-leads.md`, `05-key-frameworks.md`
**Related:** [[core-four]], [[rule-of-100]], [[more-better-new]], [[four-constraints]]

---

## The Problem They Solve

The [[core-four]] can be run by one person indefinitely — but a solopreneur running the Core Four alone is capped by their time. Lead getters break that cap.

**The multiplier:** 4 lead-getter types × 4 Core Four channels = **16 distinct lead-acquisition surfaces**. Most businesses run 1–2 of these 16.

---

## The Four Types

### 1. Customers
Your existing customers generate new leads via:
- **Referrals** — 1-to-1 warm introductions (warmest leads possible)
- **Reviews** — 1-to-many free content (Google, App Store, Trustpilot)
- **Word of mouth** — organic social amplification

**Incentive levers:** referral bonuses, "bring a friend" campaigns, referral contests, member-of-the-month recognition.

**Economics:** near-zero cost, highest-quality leads, unlimited ceiling if customer satisfaction is high.

### 2. Employees
Internal team members dedicated to one Core Four channel:
- **SDRs / BDRs** — cold outreach at scale
- **Content creators** — free content production
- **Paid media buyers** — ads management
- **Account managers** — warm reactivation of past customers

**Incentive levers:** base salary + variable comp tied to lead volume and quality.

### 3. Agencies
Outsourced execution of any Core Four channel.
- Ad agencies, cold outreach agencies, content agencies, influencer agencies, PR firms

**Tradeoffs:**
| Pros | Cons |
|---|---|
| Faster ramp to scale | Less control over quality |
| No internal headcount | Per-unit cost is higher |
| Specialized expertise | Institutional learning stays at the agency |

### 4. Affiliates
External partners with their own audiences who promote your offer for commission.
- Newsletter operators, podcasters, niche content creators, fitness influencers, complementary businesses (e.g., a supplement brand recommending your app)

**Incentive levers:** generous rev share (30–50% for digital), lifetime tracking cookies, white-glove enablement (provide them with scripts, landing pages, tracking links).

## The Scaling Sequence

| Stage | Best Lead Getter to Add First |
|---|---|
| Solopreneur | Customers (referral system) |
| First hires | Employee (one channel owner) |
| $1M+ ARR | Agencies (parallelize channels) |
| Strong brand | Affiliates (compound on brand equity) |

---

## Why Channel Diversification Matters

Relying on a single lead getter (or a single Core Four channel) makes the business fragile:
- Algorithm changes kill content reach overnight
- One key employee leaving dries up the pipeline
- An affiliate partnership ending removes a revenue stream

Sophisticated businesses run all four simultaneously with no single source accounting for >50% of leads.

---

## Application to Fitness / Workout Software

| Lead Getter | Specific Implementation |
|---|---|
| Customers | In-app referral program: "Give 1 month free, get 1 month free" |
| Employees | Hire one content creator to post workout programming content daily |
| Agencies | Contract a cold email agency to reach boutique gym owners |
| Affiliates | Partner with fitness YouTubers/Instagrammers on rev share for app sign-ups |
