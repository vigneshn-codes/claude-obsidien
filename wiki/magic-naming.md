# MAGIC Naming Formula

**Summary:** A five-element formula for naming offers, programs, and lead magnets so they pre-sell themselves before any copy is read.

**Category:** Offer Design
**Sources:** `20-concept-magic-naming.md`, `01-100m-offers.md`, `05-key-frameworks.md`
**Related:** [[grand-slam-offer]], [[bonuses-stack]], [[starving-crowd]]

---

## The Acronym

| Letter | Element | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **M** | Make a magnetic reason | "Why now?" — seasonal hook, milestone, frustration |
| **A** | Announce the avatar | Name the audience explicitly |
| **G** | Give them a goal | The dream outcome, in concrete language |
| **I** | Indicate a timeframe | Makes the result feel imminent |
| **C** | Complete with a container word | The package archetype signals intensity/format |

---

## Container Word Guide

The container word sets buyer expectation before they read a single sentence of copy. Pick the one that matches your actual delivery format.

| Container | Signal | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| **Challenge** | Short, fun, transformational | 30-day sprints, habit formation |
| **Bootcamp** | Intense, immersive, structured | Skills-based, fast-ramp programs |
| **Intensive** | Short, deep, high-touch | Weekend events, deep-dives |
| **Accelerator** | Medium-long, outcomes-focused | Cohort programs, structured curricula |
| **Blueprint** | DIY, framework-heavy | Self-paced, reference material |
| **Mastermind** | Peer-driven, high-status | Groups of peers at similar levels |
| **Sprint** | Focused, time-boxed, specific outcome | Single-skill or single-problem focus |
| **Program** | Generic, safe, familiar | When format genuinely doesn't fit above |

**The Mismatch Trap:** Calling something a "mastermind" when it's a course erodes trust faster than weak copy.

---

## Why It Works

A well-named offer answers four pre-objections in the headline alone:

1. **Is this for me?** → (Avatar)
2. **What will I get?** → (Goal)
3. **How long?** → (Timeframe)
4. **Is this a real thing or a vague idea?** → (Container)

That's most of the qualification work done before the prospect reads a single word of sales copy.

## Worked Examples

### Fitness / Workout
- *"The 6-Week Stubborn Belly Fat Challenge for Busy Moms"*
  - M: stubborn belly fat (visceral frustration) / A: busy moms / G: lose belly fat / I: 6 weeks / C: Challenge
- *"The 12-Week First Pull-Up Accelerator for Office Workers Over 30"*
- *"The 30-Day Mobility Sprint for Desk Workers With Chronic Back Pain"*
- *"The 90-Day Powerlifting Blueprint for Gym Beginners"*

### Software / App
- *"The 8-Week Muscle-Building App for Men Who Missed the Gym Window in Their 20s"*
- *"The 6-Week Comeback Program for Runners Returning After Injury"*

---

## The Full Formula in One Sentence

> [Magnetic Reason] + [Avatar] + [Goal] + [Timeframe] + [Container]

You don't always need all five elements in the title (some belong in the subtitle or tagline) — but running through all five during brainstorming forces clarity about who you're serving, what you're promising, and how you're delivering it.
