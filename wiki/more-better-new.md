# More, Better, New

**Summary:** The three-knob diagnostic for any growth problem — always exhaust "More" and "Better" before adding "New."

**Category:** Lead Generation
**Sources:** `18-concept-more-better-new.md`, `02-100m-leads.md`, `05-key-frameworks.md`
**Related:** [[core-four]], [[rule-of-100]], [[lead-getters]], [[four-constraints]]

---

## The Three Knobs

When stuck on growth (leads, revenue, output), there are only three options:

| Knob | Definition | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **More** | Do more of what you're already doing | Send 100 cold emails/day instead of 20 |
| **Better** | Do what you're doing, more effectively | Improve cold email open rate from 15% to 35% |
| **New** | Add a completely new strategy or channel | Start paid ads after cold email is maxed |

**These are the only three.** Every "what should I do next?" question routes through this framework.

---

## The Default Order

1. **More first.** Most under-performance comes from under-volume, not bad strategy.
2. **Better second.** Once at right volume, optimize conversion, copy, and process.
3. **New last.** Only after both are genuinely maxed.

Adding a *New* channel before exhausting the current one spreads attention across two underperforming channels instead of one fully-optimized one.

---

## How to Know When a Channel Is "Maxed"

A channel is maxed when all three are true:
1. You're hitting daily volume targets consistently
2. Conversion rates have plateaued for 4+ consecutive weeks despite iteration
3. Next marginal hour on this channel yields lower ROI than starting a new one

This bar is high by design — most founders declare "maxed" far too early.

---

## Worked Diagnostics

### Lead Problem
> "My cold email brings 3 leads/week"

- **More check:** Are you sending 100+ emails/day? → If no, send more. Stop here.
- **Better check:** If yes — are open rates above 40%? Reply rates above 5%? → Iterate subject line and opener.
- **New:** Only if both are genuinely maxed → add cold DM as a second channel.

### Revenue Problem
> "Revenue is flat month over month"

- **More customers** (lead gen — [[core-four]])
- **Better conversion / LTV** (offer + delivery — [[value-equation]], [[trim-and-stack]])
- **New revenue stream** (upsell, downsell, cross-sell — [[cac-payback]])

---

## The Counter-Trap

> Constantly adding "New" is FOMO disguised as ambition.

Discipline means staying on *More* and *Better* longer than feels comfortable. The discomfort of repetition is the signal you're doing it right, not wrong.

---

## Application to Fitness Software

| Current Action | More | Better | New |
|---|---|---|---|
| Posting workout content 3x/week | → 1x/day | → Optimize hooks; test different formats | → Start YouTube after Instagram is maxed |
| 20 DMs/day to gym-goers | → 100 DMs/day | → Test opener variations | → Add cold email to gym owners |
| $50/day in Meta ads | → $500/day | → A/B test creatives; refine targeting | → Add Google ads |
