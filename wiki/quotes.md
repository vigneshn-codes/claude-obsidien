## On Offers & Value

- "Make people an offer so good they would feel stupid saying no." → [[grand-slam-offer]]
- "The offer is the lifeblood of a business. The better the offer, the better the business."
- "Don't be afraid to charge a lot. The better the offer, the more you can charge."
- "If you can solve a person's problem in a way no one else can, they have nowhere else to go."
- "Sell results, not memberships." → [[six-week-challenge]]
- "Most businesses don't have a marketing problem; they have an offer problem." → [[grand-slam-offer]]

## On Leads & Marketing

- "Leads are the lifeblood. The offer is the heart."
- "Volume negates luck." → [[rule-of-100]]
- "If you're struggling to get leads, make an amazing lead magnet." → [[lead-magnet]]
- "Make the lead magnet so good they feel stupid not taking it for free."
- "More leads, lower CAC, higher LTV. Pick two. Or solve for all three." → [[cac-payback]]
- "Engagement is the only lead metric that matters. The rest is vanity."

## On Money & Pricing

- "If you make more from a customer in the first 30 days than it cost to acquire them, you can outspend every competitor — forever." → [[cac-payback]]
- "Premium pricing creates better clients, better margins, and better signals of quality."
- "The cheapest customers complain the most. The most expensive customers complain the least."
- "One of the truest laws in business: the more money you make other people, the more money you make."

## On Hard Work & Persistence

- "I cannot lose if I do not quit."
- "Set a timer for 20 hours. Actually try for 20 hours before you quit."
- "99% of the things I've learned, I've learned through doing, not thinking."
- "Outwork your self-doubt."
- "You don't become confident by shouting affirmations. You become confident by having undeniable proof you are who you say you are."

## On Mindset

- "The winner's mindset sits in the uncomfortable place between two surface-level contradictions: extreme paranoia in the present and unshakeable faith in the future."
- "We don't rise to the standards we have when others are watching; we fall to the standards we have when no one is." → [[mindset-identity]]
- "Boredom is the price of mastery."
- "Discipline is choosing between what you want now and what you want most."

## On Focus & Decision-Making

- "The cost of not deciding is greater than the cost of deciding wrong."
- "Focus is saying no to one hundred good ideas to say yes to the one great one."
- "You can have anything you want — you just can't have everything you want."
- "If you're not embarrassed by the first version, you launched too late."

## On Skill Acquisition

- "Skill acquisition is the highest-leverage activity." → [[skill-stacking]]
- "Your income is a lagging indicator of the skills you've acquired."
- "Read a book. Watch a video. Take a course. Then ship something. Repeat for a decade."

## On Leverage

- "Time is the only thing you can never get back. Buy it back ruthlessly."
- "The four leverages: labor, capital, code, content. Stack them in that order." → [[mindset-identity]]
- "If you do it once, do it manually. If you do it twice, document it. If you do it three times, automate it. If you do it four times, hire someone for it."

## On Customers

- "Put the customer first, and the rest follows."
- "Your best marketing is delivering a result so good your customers can't shut up about it."
- "Retention is the cheapest acquisition channel." → [[cac-payback]]
