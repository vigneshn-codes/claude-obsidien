# Rule of 100

**Summary:** Commit to 100 primary advertising actions every day for 100 days — the minimum effective dose to know whether a channel works.

**Category:** Lead Generation
**Sources:** `10-concept-rule-of-100.md`, `02-100m-leads.md`, `05-key-frameworks.md`
**Related:** [[core-four]], [[more-better-new]], [[lead-getters]]

---

## The Rule

Pick one of the following and do it **every day for 100 days**:

- Reach out to **100 people**
- Spend **100 minutes** creating content
- Spend **$100** on ads

Do not mix channels when you're starting. Pick one. Do it for 100 days.

---

## The Premise

> "If you reach out to 100 people *total* instead of 100 per day, you're doing 1/42 of the required effort."

Most "this doesn't work" claims come from dramatically under-dosing the input. The channel isn't broken — the volume is.

---

## Why 100?

**100 actions/day:**
- High enough that randomness averages out (statistically significant sample)
- Low enough that one person can sustain it solo
- Round enough to measure and remember

**100 days:**
- Long enough for cold pipelines to ripen (most outreach converts 2–8 weeks later)
- Long enough for content to compound (early posts get discovered later)
- Long enough to disqualify the "I tried it once" claim

---

## Stage-Appropriate Dosing

| Stage | Recommended Commitment |
|---|---|
| Founder, no budget | 100 outreach messages/day (DM or email) |
| Founder + small audience | 100 minutes of content creation/day |
| Funded business | $100/day in ads (minimum entry point) |
| Mature business | All three, at 10× volume, run by [[lead-getters]] |

---

## Anti-Patterns

- **Counting attempts, not contacts** — sent ≠ delivered ≠ opened ≠ engaged
- **Channel mixing** (50 emails + 50 DMs) when starting — pick one channel for clarity before adding a second
- **Stopping at day 30** because "nobody's biting" — most channels reward consistency, not polish; the pipeline is just warming up
- **Optimizing copy before hitting volume** — write a decent message, send 100, then optimize; not write a perfect message, send 5, declare failure

---

## The Companion Principle: Volume Negates Luck

> "Volume negates luck."

A large enough sample size erases dependence on any single outcome. One bad day of cold email doesn't matter when there are 99 more days of data. One flop video doesn't matter when you're posting 90 more.

Hormozi treats this as a law, not a suggestion.

## Application to Fitness / Workout Niche

| Goal | Daily Action |
|---|---|
| Find first 100 beta users for workout app | DM 100 gym-going people on Instagram with a relevant account |
| Build a content following | 100 minutes filming/editing workout content daily |
| Generate gym owner leads for software | 100 minutes of cold email outreach to gym owners |
| Test paid acquisition | $100/day to Meta targeting gym-goers in your zip code |
