# Scarcity & Urgency

**Summary:** Two distinct psychological levers that compress the buyer's decision window — scarcity limits supply, urgency limits time.

**Category:** Offer Design
**Sources:** `14-concept-scarcity-urgency.md`, `01-100m-offers.md`
**Related:** [[grand-slam-offer]], [[bonuses-stack]], [[guarantee-taxonomy]]

---

## The Distinction

| | Scarcity | Urgency |
|---|---|---|
| **What it limits** | Supply (how many can have it) | Time (when they can get it) |
| **Example** | "Only 10 coaching spots" | "Enrollment closes Friday" |
| **Psychology** | Fear of missing out on access | Fear of missing the deadline |

Both tap loss aversion — which is roughly 2× as psychologically powerful as equivalent gain. Making the cost of *not deciding* concrete and imminent overcomes inertia.

---

## Scarcity Patterns

- **Limited seats** — "Only 10 clients per cohort; I personally coach each one"
- **Limited inventory** — "200 founding member slots at this price"
- **Limited bonuses** — "First 50 buyers get the live Q&A add-on"
- **Limited access tiers** — "Only 3 advisory slots this quarter"

---

## Urgency Patterns

- **Rolling cohorts** — "This cohort starts Monday; next opening is March"
- **Pricing deadlines** — "Price increases to $X on the 1st"
- **Exploding bonuses** — "The done-for-you setup expires at midnight"
- **Seasonal urgency** — "New Year intake closes Jan 15; next window is summer"
- **Personalized urgency** — "Your custom plan expires in 72 hours"

---

## The "Reason Why" Rule

Every scarcity or urgency claim **must come with a believable, structural reason.**

| Bad | Good |
|---|---|
| "Only 10 spots available!" (feels invented) | "I personally coach every client in this cohort — I can only manage 10 at a time" (anchored to real constraint) |
| "Offer expires tonight!" (arbitrary) | "This pricing is valid during our launch window — we increase prices after the first 100 customers to match our higher delivery costs" |

Without a credible reason, scarcity reads as manipulation and *decreases* trust with sophisticated buyers.

---

## Combining Scarcity and Urgency

Stacked, they compound:

> *"Only 25 founding member slots (scarcity), enrollment closes Friday midnight (urgency), and the Plateau Protocol bonus expires Wednesday (urgency + bonus scarcity)."*

**The rule:** one credible constraint beats three implausible ones. Never invent multiple deadlines that conflict with each other or contradict the offer logic.

---

## The Structural Scarcity Principle

Hormozi's recommended long-term approach: build the business so scarcity is **structurally real**.

- Cohort-based delivery (capacity genuinely limited by facilitator time)
- Advisory seats (1:1 time is finite)
- Done-for-you services (bandwidth caps)
- Live events (venue capacity)

Structural scarcity is self-reinforcing: no fabrication required, no damage to brand integrity, and it naturally creates waitlists which function as free social proof.
