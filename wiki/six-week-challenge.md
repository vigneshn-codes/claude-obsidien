# Six-Week Challenge (Gym Launch Model)

**Summary:** Hormozi's signature front-end offer for gyms — a defined-duration transformation challenge with an outcome promise, a stake/refund mechanic, and a built-in conversion to membership at the end.

**Category:** Fitness / Gym Applications
**Sources:** `04-gym-launch-secrets.md`, `24-company-gym-launch.md`
**Related:** [[grand-slam-offer]], [[value-equation]], [[scarcity-urgency]], [[money-model]], [[magic-naming]]

---

## What It Is

A **6-week challenge** is a time-boxed front-end transformation offer used to:
1. Acquire new gym members at high conversion (people buy outcomes, not memberships)
2. Deliver a quick win that creates believers
3. Convert challengers into long-term members at the end of the 6 weeks

It was the offer that built Gym Launch from 0 to $46.2M valuation and has been replicated by thousands of gyms worldwide.

---

## Why It Works (Value Equation Lens)

| Variable | How the 6-Week Challenge Addresses It |
|---|---|
| Dream Outcome | Concrete ("Lose X lbs," "Finally do a pull-up") — not "get fit" |
| Perceived Likelihood | Short duration feels more achievable; outcome is specific; refund mechanics reduce risk |
| Time Delay | 6 weeks is short enough to feel fast; first-session win is built in |
| Effort & Sacrifice | Group context + accountability removes willpower dependency |

## The Structure

### Phase 1 — The Offer (Before They Sign Up)
- Specific transformation promise: "Lose 14 lbs in 6 weeks or your money back"
- Named program: *"The 6-Week Body Transformation Challenge"* (MAGIC formula)
- A stake/refund mechanic that filters committed buyers (conditional guarantee)
- Scarcity: "Only 20 spots — starts [date]"
- Upfront payment (maximizes CAC payback)

### Phase 2 — The First Session (Day 1 Win)
- The point of the first session is not to train — it's to deliver confidence
- New member must leave Day 1 feeling they can do this
- Onboarding rituals: welcome, photo, baseline measurement, community introduction

### Phase 3 — The 6 Weeks
- Group sessions (scalable delivery)
- Weekly check-ins and accountability (retention mechanic)
- Public progress tracking (community + social proof)
- Coach-driven reactivation if someone misses a session

### Phase 4 — The Conversion (End of Week 6)
- Built-in sales conversation at the end
- Prospect has already experienced results → conversion is much easier
- Offer: continue as a regular member at the monthly rate
- Upsell to higher-tier coaching or specialty programs

## The Money Model Behind It

```
Challenge upfront payment ($197–$397) → Recovers ad spend (CAC)
End-of-challenge membership conversion → Monthly recurring revenue (LTV)
Upsell to coaching tier → Higher LTV
```

The challenge is the front-end. The membership is the back-end. See [[money-model]] and [[cac-payback]].

## Transferability Beyond Gyms

The 6-week challenge model generalizes to any business where:
- The prospect needs a defined transformation outcome
- The result is achievable in a short time horizon
- Accountability improves results
- A recurring purchase makes sense after the initial transformation

| Industry | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Workout app | "30-Day App Challenge" → convert to annual subscription |
| Online coaching | "6-Week Online Program" → convert to ongoing coaching |
| Nutrition | "4-Week Meal Prep Challenge" → convert to meal plan subscription |
| Corporate wellness | "6-Week Desk Worker Mobility Challenge" → convert to company license |

## Designing Your Own Challenge Offer

1. Name it using [[magic-naming]]: *"The [X]-Week [Transformation] Challenge for [Avatar]"*
2. Promise a specific, measurable outcome
3. Add a conditional [[guarantee-taxonomy]]: "Complete every session, hit the outcome or full refund"
4. Limit spots with structural [[scarcity-urgency]] (cohort-based delivery)
5. Build Day-1 win into the program design
6. Schedule the membership conversion conversation at week 5, not week 6
