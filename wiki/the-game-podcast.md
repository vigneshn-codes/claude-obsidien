## Overview

- **Host:** Alex Hormozi
- **Launched:** ~2020
- **Format:** Solo monologues (primarily), occasional interviews
- **Typical Length:** 5–45 minutes
- **Distribution:** Apple Podcasts, Spotify, YouTube
- **Promise:** "How to make more money. How to keep more of it. How to enjoy it more."

## Relationship to the Books

The $100M trilogy is essentially the *best of* the podcast — polished and structured. The podcast back catalog functions as an extended commentary track on the books:

- $100M Offers borrows from earlier pricing/offer episodes
- $100M Leads echoes years of lead-gen monologues
- $100M Money Models pulls from unit-economics discussions across hundreds of episodes

For deep engagement: read the books for structure, listen to the podcast for raw examples and edge cases.

---

## Production Philosophy

- No sponsors, no intros, no fluff
- Often recorded between meetings in hotels or offices
- Edited tight — dead air and tangents cut aggressively
- Show notes minimal; the audio is the artifact

This is a direct application of [[value-equation]]: maximize value per second, minimize effort and time delay.

---

## Strategic Role for Acquisition.com

The podcast is top-of-funnel:
1. Builds trust over hundreds of free hours
2. Pre-qualifies sophisticated founders
3. Drives book sales and inbound advisory deal flow
4. Compounds over years — long-tail discovery on every episode

The content is the front-end. Advisory and equity are the back-end. See [[money-model]].
