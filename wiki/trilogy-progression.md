## The Three-Book Architecture

| # | Book | Year | Core Question | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **$100M Offers** | 2021 | What do I sell, and how do I package it so it sells itself? | Irresistible **offer** |
| 2 | **$100M Leads** | 2023 | How do I get strangers to discover my offer? | Scalable **lead system** |
| 3 | **$100M Money Models** | 2025 | How do I get paid enough, fast enough, to outspend competitors? | Compounding **money model** |

**The composition formula:**
```
Offer × Leads = Revenue
(Offer × Leads) × Money Model = Compounding Business
```

---

## How They Depend on Each Other

A great offer with no leads is a private secret. Many leads with a bad offer is a leaky funnel. A working funnel that loses money on the first sale can't scale without external capital. All three must work.

---

## Concept Threads Running Through All Three

### Thread 1 — Perceived Value
- **Offers:** introduces the [[value-equation]]
- **Leads:** [[lead-magnet]] design obeys the Value Equation (must be something people would pay for)
- **Money Models:** pricing tiers designed to maximize perceived value per dollar

### Thread 2 — Volume
- **Offers:** [[divergent-thinking]] (volume of ideas → trim to best offer)
- **Leads:** [[rule-of-100]] (volume of actions → leads)
- **Money Models:** volume of customers × reinvestment loop → compounding growth

### Thread 3 — Risk Reversal
- **Offers:** [[guarantee-taxonomy]] moves risk to the seller
- **Leads:** [[lead-magnet]] gives value before asking for anything
- **Money Models:** low-friction front-end reduces buyer risk at entry

## Reading Order by Situation

| Reader Type | Recommended Order | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-revenue founder | Offers → Leads → Money Models | Build the offer first, then fill the pipeline |
| Established founder ($1M+) | Money Models first → back-fill | Diagnose binding constraint ([[four-constraints]]), then address root cause |
| Marketer / operator (not founder) | Leads → Offers → Money Models | Lead gen is the immediate job; offer design and unit economics are strategic |

---

## What Each Book Is *Not* About

| Book | Not About |
|---|---|
| $100M Offers | Marketing channels, lead generation |
| $100M Leads | Offer design, pricing, packaging |
| $100M Money Models | Personal finance, savings, investing |

Confusing scope is the most common reason readers report disappointment with a single book.
