# Trim & Stack

**Summary:** A two-step surgical method for designing the deliverable side of an offer — cut what costs you a lot and delivers little perceived value; add what costs you little and delivers high perceived value.

**Category:** Offer Design
**Sources:** `12-concept-trim-and-stack.md`, `01-100m-offers.md`
**Related:** [[grand-slam-offer]], [[value-equation]], [[bonuses-stack]], [[divergent-thinking]]

---

## The Core Mechanic

Most offers are accidental — they accumulate features by reflex ("let's throw in a call," "let's add a PDF"). Trim & Stack imposes a 2×2 ROI lens.

|   | **Low cost to you** | **High cost to you** |
|---|---|---|
| **High value to customer** | ★ **STACK these** | Keep selectively — justify the cost |
| **Low value to customer** | Optional filler — cut | ✂ **TRIM these immediately** |

---

## What to Trim

| Deliverable Type | Why It's a Trim Candidate |
|---|---|
| Unlimited 1-on-1 calls | Caps your time; client rarely uses most of them |
| Bespoke deliverables that don't scale | Each one is net new cost |
| Long setup / onboarding phases | Raises effort & sacrifice before any value lands |
| Custom reports nobody opens | High production cost, near-zero perceived value |
| "We'll figure it out together" elements | Vague value, high delivery friction |

---

## What to Stack

| Deliverable Type | Why It Stacks |
|---|---|
| Templates and swipe files | Near-zero marginal cost; saves buyer hours |
| Software / automation / app | One build, infinite delivery |
| Group coaching calls | 10× the clients per hour; community adds value |
| Community access | Members provide value to each other |
| Recordings of past cohorts | Sunk cost to produce; perpetual value |
| Pre-built playbooks | Replaces custom builds; faster for the buyer |
| Bonus mini-modules | Solve specific objections; cheap to record once |

---

## The Mental Workflow

1. **Diverge first** — brainstorm 20+ possible deliverables (see [[divergent-thinking]]). No judgment yet.
2. **Score each** on the 2×2.
3. **Trim** the bottom-right (high cost, low value).
4. **Stack** the top-left (low cost, high value).
5. **Re-bundle.** Rename with [[magic-naming]]. Reprice.

---

## The Counter-Intuitive Insight

**Removing things often raises perceived value.** When you remove low-value deliverables that required buyer effort (weekly homework assignments, complex setup, prerequisite courses), you're reducing the "Effort & Sacrifice" variable in the [[value-equation]]. The offer gets more valuable by shrinking.

A lean offer with five high-value pieces beats a sprawling offer with twenty mediocre ones.

---

## Application to Workout Software / App Products

| What to Trim | What to Stack |
|---|---|
| Manual check-in DMs from coaches | Automated progress tracking in-app |
| Bespoke program design per user | Algorithm-generated progressive overload |
| Lengthy intake calls | 3-question onboarding quiz |
| Custom nutrition plans per user | Pre-built macro templates by goal type |
| Daily email drips with homework | Short in-app reminders + streak tracking |

The result: users get the *perceived outcome* (personalized program) at a fraction of the delivery cost.
